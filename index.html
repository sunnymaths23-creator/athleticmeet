Cricket scoreboard & Net Run Rate

NRR = runs scored per over − runs conceded per over. If a team is all out before completing its allotted overs, use the full quota of overs for NRR as per standard limited-overs calculation. Enter completed overs precisely, e.g. 17.4 means 17 overs and 4 balls, not 17.4 decimal overs.
